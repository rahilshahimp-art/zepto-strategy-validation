# Zepto customer survey: questionnaire

**Purpose.** Test whether the strategy agreed at the leadership review of 9 November 2026 (safer delivery, fresher stock, a path to profit) is sustainable with customers. Target: adults in Indian cities who used a quick-commerce app in the last three months. About 5 minutes.

Each question is there because one of the four algorithms needs it.

| Algorithm | Business question it answers | Questions used |
|---|---|---|
| Regression | What drives how much a customer spends each month? | Target Q24; predictors Q1, Q3, Q5, Q6, Q12, Q13, Q18 |
| Classification | Who will pay for a membership? | Target Q23; predictors Q1, Q3, Q5, Q6, Q7, Q8, Q12, Q13, Q15, Q18 |
| Clustering | What customer types exist, and which are worth most? | Q1, Q5, Q6, Q7, Q8, Q12, Q13, Q24 |
| Association rules | Which product categories are bought together? | Q11 |
| Descriptive and diagnostic checks | Do customers accept each strategy decision? | Q9, Q10, Q14-Q17, Q19-Q22, Q25, Q26 |

## Section A: About you

5. **Monthly household income** in rupees. *(number)*

## Section B: How you shop
7. **How many quick-commerce orders** do you place in a typical month? *(number)*
8. **Average value of one order** in rupees. *(number)*
9. **Which app do you use most?** Zepto / Blinkit / Instamart / Other
10. **When do you usually order?** Morning / Afternoon / Evening / Late night
11. **Which categories did you buy in the last month?** *(tick all that apply)* Fruits & Vegetables / Dairy & Bread / Staples / Snacks / Beverages / Instant Food / Household Cleaning / Personal Care / Baby Care / Pet Care / Electronics Accessories / Pharma & Wellness

## Section C: What you think (1 = lowest, 5 = highest)

13. How **sensitive are you to price** and fees?
14. How **satisfied are you with freshness** of fruit, vegetables and dairy?
15. How **often are items you want out of stock**?
16. How **concerned are you about rider safety**?
17. How do you rate the **app experience**?
18. **Overall satisfaction** with your main app.

## Section D: Your reaction to proposed changes

20. What is the **highest delivery fee you would pay on a small order**, in rupees? *(number)*
21. If free delivery needed a **minimum order of Rs 199**, what would you do? Already order above Rs 199 / Add items to reach Rs 199 / Pay the delivery fee / Switch to another app / Order less often
22. Would you use a **"Fresh Deals" evening discount** section for near-expiry items? Yes / Maybe / No
23. Would you **join a Rs 99 per month membership** for free delivery and member offers? Yes / No
24. **How much do you spend** on quick commerce in a typical month, in rupees? *(number)*
25. How likely are you to **recommend your main app** to a friend? *(0 to 10)*
26. Do you expect to **still be using it in six months**? Yes / No
